2 Your decks

How to read the numbers: the estimates come from a model that learns from recorded battle results; it doesn't play the game. Picking the top of many estimates makes the top ones look a little better than they are, and the 90% ranges don't include that. A deck's warnings and its exact-deck record matter more than a small gap between two estimates.
