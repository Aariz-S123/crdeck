How good is the model?

  • Win rates describe top players. They are estimates against the opponent decks seen in the test window, at equal player rating. They are not a promise about your games.
  • Top results run optimistic. The best of many noisy estimates is biased upward. The interval covers model and sampling uncertainty, not that selection effect.
  • Synergy is correlation. A positive score means two cards won together more than expected from each one alone in this data. It is not proof of a combo.
  • Unseen decks are extrapolated. “Unseen deck” means nobody in the collected battles played these exact 8 cards; the score comes from the cards and their pairs.
  • Legality is a hard rule. Every deck respects 8 cards, one form per card, and the Evolution / Hero / Wild slots (two champions allowed, as seen in real ranked decks). Warnings never block a deck.
  • Archetype labels come from a classifier trained on 88 decks labeled by the project's assistant; below 50% confidence it says “Hybrid / uncertain”.
