Balance watch

This page lists cards that look out of line on the numbers Supercell says it watches: how often a card is used, and how often decks with it win. A card on this list is not certain to change, and cards change for reasons this data can't see, like how they feel to play against. We save every list before each update and check it afterwards; that track record is at the bottom.

Tiers, never probabilities: a listed card is one that looks out of line in recorded battles, not one that is sure to change. A fan-made learning project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Supercell. Battle data from the official Clash Royale API.
