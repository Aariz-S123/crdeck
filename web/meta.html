Card usage trends

What the sampled Ranked (Path of Legends) players use, and how that changed between data collections. People call this the “meta”: what is commonly played right now. This page always uses Ranked battles.

Usage share: the share of Ranked deck appearances in a period that include the card form. History ranges are 90% bootstrap intervals; forecast bands come from the Monte Carlo runs. A fan-made learning project. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Supercell. Battle data from the official Clash Royale API.
